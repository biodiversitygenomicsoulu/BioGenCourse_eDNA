## Method 1: Coffee-Column
Overview: Filter water through a coffee filter, then extract DNA using spin columns. Best for maximum species detection when time and lab equipment are available.

### Field Equipment
| Item | Quantity (per site) |
| --- | --- |
| Coffee filter (standard, unbleached, conical) | 2+ filters (minimum: 1 sample and 1 negative control) |
| Coffee filter pour-over funnel | 1 funnel per site |
| 10 L sturdy bucket | 1 bucket per site |

### Laboratory Equipment
| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Centrifuge | Spin column extraction (capable of 13000 x g) |
| Heat block(s) or water baths | Incubation steps (55°C, 70°C) |
| Microcentrifuge tubes (1.5–2.0 mL) | Sample storage and extraction |
| Pipettes (P20, P200, P1000) | Liquid transfer |
| Pipette tips | Liquid transfer |
| Vortex mixer | Mixing samples |
| Sterile petri dishes | Processing coffee filter membranes |
| Forceps | Handling coffee filter membranes |

### Laboratory Consumables
| Item | Quantity (per sample) | Storage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E.Z.N.A.® Tissue DNA Kit (Omega Bio-Tek; cat. no. D3396) | 1 kit | Room temperature | Contains spin columns, buffers, proteinase K |
| Ethanol (96–100%) | Variable | Room temperature | Dilute DNA Wash Buffer concentrate prior to first use, according to kit instructions |
| Isopropanol (100%) | Variable | Room temperature | Dilute HBC Buffer concentrate prior to first use, according to kit instructions |
| 10% bleach solution | Variable | Room temperature | Decontamination of all equipment and surfaces |
| Ethanol (70%) | Variable | Room temperature | To remove residual bleach and facilitate drying |

### Field Procedure
#### 1. Sample collection
> **Note:** Change single-use gloves between samples
1. Decontaminate bucket, coffee funnel, and filter handling equipment (e.g., forceps, scissors, etc.) using a 10% bleach solution (exposing or submerging surfaces for > 1 min). Air dry or use 70% ethanol rinse to facilitate drying and minimize oxidation/rusting.
2. Rinse sterile bucket with water from your sampling site three times; avoid disturbing sediment. Collect 10 L.
3. Place the coffee filter in the pour-over funnel.
4. Slowly pour water through the filter. Note: This takes approximately 1 hour per sample depending on water turbidity.
5. Once filtration is complete, carefully place the filter in a sterile resealable plastic bag.
6. Label the bag with sample ID, date, and location.
7. Store on ice immediately and transport to the lab.


### Laboratory Procedure: DNA Extraction (Spin Column)
#### 1. Prepare samples
1. Add 200 µL of Buffer TL and 20 µL of proteinase K from the E.Z.N.A. kit to 2.0 mL microcentrifuge tubes (one for each sample, plus a negative control).
2. Using sterile forceps, place the coffee filter in a sterile petri dish.
3. Cut the bottom of the coffee filter (as shown in Figure S2), roll carefully with sterile forceps, and place in microcentrifuge tube(s). Change gloves and sterilize all worksurfaces, forceps, scissors in 10% bleach solution (for at least 1 min) followed by 70% ethanol between samples. Metal forceps and scissors can be dipped in ethanol (96–100%) and sterilized over a burner flame.
4. Vortex sample tubes briefly to mix.


#### 2. Lysis
1. Incubate at 55°C for 1–3 hours, vortexing every 30 min.
2. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 min.
3. Transfer supernatant to a sterile 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube. Do not disturb the insoluble pellet.


#### 3. Bind DNA
1. Add 220 µL of Buffer BL and vortex to mix.
2. Incubate at 70°C for 10 minutes.
3. Add 220 µL of ethanol (96–100%) and vortex to mix.


#### 4. Spin column purification
1. Transfer the entire mixture to a spin column in a collection tube.
2. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 minute. Discard the flow-through and re-use the collection tube.
3. Add 500 µL of diluted HBC buffer to the column. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 minute. Discard the flow-through and collection tube. Insert spin column into a new collection tube.
4. Add 700 µL of diluted DNA Wash Buffer. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 minute. Discard the flow-through and re-use the collection tube.
5. Repeat step 4.
6. Centrifuge the empty column at 14,000 × g for 4 minutes to dry the column.


#### 5. Elute DNA
1. Transfer the column to a clean, labeled 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube.
2. Add 50–100 µL of Elution Buffer (preheated to 70°C) to the center of the membrane.
3. Incubate at room temperature for 2 minutes.
4. Centrifuge at maximum speed for 1 minute.
5. Store extracted DNA at –20°C.

### Laboratory Procedure: DNA Extraction (QuickExtract)
#### 1. Prepare samples
1. Using sterile forceps, place the coffee filter in a sterile petri dish.
2. Cut the bottom of the coffee filter into small pieces and place in a 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tube (as shown in Figure S2). Change gloves and sterilize all worksurfaces, forceps, scissors in 10% bleach solution (for at least 1 min) followed by 70% ethanol between samples. Metal forceps and scissors can be dipped in ethanol (96–100%) and sterilized over a burner flame.
3. Add 300-500 µL of QuickExtract DNA Extraction Solution (to entirely cover the filter pieces).
4. Vortex samples briefly to mix.


#### 2. Heat extraction
1. Incubate at 56°C for 2 minutes.
2. Vortex for 5 seconds.
3. Incubate at 56°C for 13 minutes.
4. Vortex for 5 seconds.
5. Incubate at 98°C for 5 minutes.
6. Vortex for 5 seconds.
7. Store extracted DNA at –20°C.


> **Note:** QuickExtract produces DNA that is ready for PCR without further purification. However, it is less sensitive than spin column extraction for rare taxa.

### Field Procedure: PEDS Deployment
#### 1. Assemble PEDS sampler
> **Note:** See Nichols et al. (2025) for more detailed information on PEDS assembly and use
1. Cut a piece of nylon mesh to approximately 20 × 10 cm. Fold in half to create a 10 x 10 pouch.
2. Using sterile gloves and forceps, place one cotton pad in the center of the mesh pouch.
3. Fold the mesh over the cotton pad and secure with cable ties at all four corners, plus one to close the mesh opening and secure sampler in the field.
4. Attach the sampler to a rope with a float at the top and a weight at the bottom.
5. Bring negative control PEDS into the field and process alongside biological samples.


#### 2. Deploy sampler
1. Suspend the PEDS sampler in the water column at the desired depth.
2. Leave deployed for at least 15 minutes (and up to 24–48 hours as determined by your study design).


#### 3. Retrieve sampler
1. After deployment, carefully remove the sampler from the water.
2. Place the entire sampler (cotton pad + mesh) in a sterile, labeled, resealable plastic bag.
3. Store on ice immediately and transport to the lab.

3. Cut the cotton pad into small pieces (as shown in Figure S2) and place in a 2.0 mL microcentrifuge tube.
4. Vortex samples briefly to mix.

## Method 5: Sylphium-Sylphium
Overview: Fully enclosed filtration system with dedicated extraction kit. Lowest contamination risk, but most expensive and requires specialized equipment.

### Field Equipment
| Item | Quantity (per sample) | Storage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sylphium complete filtration kit (Sylphium Molecular Ecology; cat. no. SYL009) | 2+ filters (minimum: 1 sample and 1 negative control) | Room temperature | Kit includes capsules, 60 mL syringe, storage/lysis buffer |
| 10 L sturdy bucket | 1 bucket |  | To simplify water collection, sterilize prior to use |

### Laboratory Equipment
| Item | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Fume hood | Ventilation for phase-separation chloroform extraction step |
| Centrifuge with 15 mL conical tube adapter | Sample extraction (capable of 13000 x g and refrigeration ≤ 4°C) |
| Incubator | Optional: for reducing overnight lysis time (55–60°C) |
| 15 mL conical sample tubes | Sample extraction |
| Microcentrifuge tubes (1.5–2.0 mL) | Sample storage and extraction |
| Pipettes (P20, P200, P1000) | Liquid transfer |
| Filtered pipette tips | Prevent contamination |
| Vortex mixer | Mixing samples |

### Laboratory Consumables
| Item | Quantity (per sample) | Storage | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sylphium eDNA Extraction Kit (Sylphium Molecular Ecology; cat. no. SYL002) | 1 kit | Room temperature | Contains all necessary buffers for extraction |

### Field Procedure
#### 1. Sample collection
1. Rinse sterile bucket with water from your sampling site three times; avoid disturbing sediment. Collect 10 L.
2. Label the Sylphium capsule with site metadata. Attach the Sylphium capsule to the syringe according to the kit instructions.
3. Slowly draw water through the capsule. Note: In turbid water, the capsule may clog before attaining the recommended 50 x 50 mL pumps. In clear water, filtration is faster.
4. Once filtration is complete, add the storage/lysis buffer following the kit instructions, cap the capsule and place both capsule and syringe in the same resealable plastic bag.
5. Label the bag with sample ID, date, location, and approximate volume (number of pumps).
6. Store at ambient temperature and transport to the lab.


### Laboratory Procedure: DNA Extraction (Sylphium Kit)
> **Important:** All steps involving phase separation buffer (S2) must be performed in a with adequate ventilation (i.e., fume hood).
#### 1. Prepare samples
1. Incubate eDNA dual filter capsule at 55–60°C for 1 hour or let samples sit at room temperature for at least 24 hours.
2. Prepare 15 mL conical tubes with 500 µL S2 and 2 mL tubes with 100 µL S3 + 900 µL S4 for each sample, plus one control.
3. Connect the used 60 mL syringe to the filter capsule outlet, remove the cap, and slowly draw up the liquid.
4. Add the syringe contents to the labeled 15 mL conical tube containing S2.


#### 2. Lysis and extraction
1. Centrifuge samples at 5000 x g for 5 min to form a bilayer.
2. Transfer up to 1 mL of the water layer to the mixture of S3 and S4, mix by shaking and cool to –20°C for 30 min.
3. Transfer the remaining water layer (~1 mL) to a 2 mL tube as a backup and store at –20°C.
4. Centrifuge samples in a cooled centrifuge (< 4°C) at > 13000 x g for 30 min and remove liquid.
5. Add 500 µL S5, centrifuge at maximum speed for 5 min and remove the liquid. Repeat this step again.
6. Dissolve the pellet in 50 µL S6.
7. Store extracted DNA at –20°C.
